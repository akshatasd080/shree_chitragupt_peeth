Delete your Shree Chitragupt Peeth account

This page lets you request deletion of your app account and associated personal data for the Shree Chitragupt Peeth Android app (org.shreechitraguptpeeth.app).

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Shree Chitragupt Peeth app
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm

Option 2 — Request by email

Email shreechitraguptpeeth@gmail.com

Use subject: Delete my account

Include:

  • Your registered full name
  • Registered email address
  • Registered 10-digit mobile number

We will verify ownership and delete the account and associated personal data. You should receive confirmation, typically within 7–14 days.

What gets deleted

  • Account profile (name, email, mobile, login credentials)
  • Associated app account data stored for that user

We may retain limited information when required for legal compliance, security, or fraud prevention, as described in our Privacy Policy.
