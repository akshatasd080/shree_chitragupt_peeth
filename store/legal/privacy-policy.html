1. Information we collect

When you create an account or use app features, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and mobile number
  • Account credentials (password is transmitted securely and stored by our server; we do not display your password in the app)
  • Optional city / profile details you provide
  • Information you submit in forms (pooja booking, membership, donation interest, contact messages)
  • Technical logs needed to operate the service (for example, IP address and request timestamps on our servers)

2. How we use information

  • To create and manage your devotee account
  • To provide temple content (news, events, gallery, videos, aarti, daily thoughts)
  • To process bookings, membership requests, donation enquiries, and contact messages
  • To communicate about temple activities when you request information
  • To improve app reliability and security

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with service providers who host our website/API, and only as needed to operate the app. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Media downloads

If you choose to save an image or video to your device gallery, the file is downloaded and written to your gallery. The app does not require broad access to read your entire photo library.

5. Data retention

Account and form data are retained while your account is active and as needed for temple administration, legal, or fraud-prevention purposes. You may request deletion as described below.

6. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account from the app under Profile → Delete Account.

You can also request deletion outside the app:

  • Web page: Delete Account
  • Email: shreechitraguptpeeth@gmail.com with subject “Delete my account”, including your registered email or mobile number

When we delete an account, we delete associated personal data, except where retention is required for legitimate legal or security reasons, which we will disclose if applicable.

7. Children’s privacy

The app is intended for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal data without appropriate consent, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Security

We use HTTPS for API communication and industry-standard safeguards appropriate to our services. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the app after updates means you acknowledge the revised policy.
